## Implemented
- Preserved four stable stage IDs and the BF-24 Jungle delegation. Factory, City Siege and Desert Assault remain original, stage-specific procedural worlds with independent sky, depth, scenery, landmarks, hazards and material palettes. Factory receives vent/warning detail and a recessed slatted furnace structure; City gains subdued illuminated district signage; Desert concrete blocks become more open, perforated ruins. These decorations are **background only**, never collision or targetable tiles.
- Preserved authoritative live `map.tiles` 0=air, 1=soil, 2=wood, 3=stone, 4=steel, 5=volatile. Corrected an edge-rendering bug: invalid neighboring tile IDs (e.g. 99) are air, not invisible solid material suppressing a live tile's perimeter. Destroyed terrain is sampled afresh each draw and disappears immediately.
- Lazy-allocated `OffscreenCanvas` tile sprites by **active** Factory/City/Desert theme. First render now allocates 20 sprites instead of 60 for all three themes; subsequent draws reuse the theme cache. Jungle allocates none in this module. No remote assets or runtime randomness.
- Low-quality render now skips 12 ornamental sky stripes per frame, in addition to existing optional smoke/window/sign and ambient detail reductions. All scene planes and tiles retain viewport culling and camera parallax. Boss, players and helicopter should still be composited **after** this background/terrain art.

## Public exports and shape

- `BROFORCE_STAGE_IDS`: frozen ordered array `['broforce-jungle','broforce-factory','broforce-city','broforce-desert']`.
- `generateBroforceVariant({stageId,seed}={})`: rejects unrecognized IDs; preserves BF-10 `width=180`, `height=24`, `tileSize=16`, row-major `Uint8Array tiles`, normalized `seed`, `spawns` (two BF-13 12x26 top-left coordinates), four `prisoners`, nine `enemySpawns`, `devilSpawn`, `extraction` and two `checkpoints`. All positions are world pixels, +y down.
- Added: `stageId`, `theme`, `title`, `missionLabel`, `flavor`, `objective` and fresh `environment={biome,weather,landmark,structures}`. Each structure has `id,kind,x,y,width,openings` in world pixels.
- Adds fixed `extraction.id='bf-extraction'` while preserving BF-10's exact extraction coordinates.
- Titles: *Broforce: Jungle*, *Broforce: Factory*, *Broforce: City Siege*, *Broforce: Desert Assault*. Themes: `jungle`, `factory`, `city`, `desert`. Stage choice derives only from explicit `stageId`.
- Uses separate stage-ID-mixed seeded RNG, never `Math.random`; each invocation produces fresh map, actor objects, environment and structures.

## Layout design

- **Jungle:** wood relay-outpost roofs, canopy breaks and destructible wooden substrate.
- **Factory:** wood/steel catwalks, ground conveyors represented as breakable wood lanes and volatile stores below grade. Actual conveyor forces are *not* implemented by this data-only module.
- **City Siege:** tenement roofs with single-tile skylights, breakable roof/ground surfacing and reinforced stone edging.
- **Desert Assault:** low-bunker and watchtower silhouettes, rocky surfacing and lookout platforms.
- Existing BF-10 mission landmarks and main route remain the movement backbone. Each theme now also reworks BF-10's **verified traversable elevated detour** at zone five: a timber canopy extension, a reinforced conveyor bridge, a tenement skywalk, or a rocky lookout path. This guarantees a reachable variant-specific alternate route in sampled seeds. Raised architectural decks retain clearance over the main lane; additional one-step-accessed decks are added only where flat-space safety checks permit. Not every high architectural silhouette is claimed as traversable.

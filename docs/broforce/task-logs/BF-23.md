## Public API / integration protocol
- `createBroforceFx(seed=1,{quality='high',reducedMotion=false}={})`
- `emitBroforceFx(fx,{type,x,y,dx?,dy?,radius?,intensity?})` (also accepts `name` / `kind` in place of `type`)
- `advanceBroforceFx(fx,dt)` uses **active seconds**; do not call during pause
- `drawBroforceFx(ctx,{fx,camera,viewport,time=0,layer='all'}={})`; camera is unshaken world top-left, viewport has pixel width/height. Use `layer:'back'` after terrain, then actors/portraits, then `layer:'front'`, with HUD/controls rendered last. Or draw `'all'` once as a fallback.
- Apply `fx.shakeOffset.x/y` as a **single outer world-only transform** around terrain, actors and both FX draw calls; restore before HUD/input overlays. Do not double-apply shake inside `drawBroforceFx`.
- Cosmetic RNG never references gameplay RNG or `Math.random`; no DOM, audio, networking or save writes. Quality caps: high 128 particles, 10 rings, 4 flashes; low 56 / 5 / 2; reduced motion removes flash/shake and caps particles at 28 (18 in low).

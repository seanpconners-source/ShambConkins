## Design and event contract

- Pure per-encounter simulation; no game engine, graphics, sounds, asset, persistence, profile or main-game fighter changes. `characterId` MUST be chosen earlier by BF-17 (`chooseDevil`); missing id fails closed.
- Boss state stores the selected exact character ID, fixed-step 60Hz tick and remainder, deterministic seeded RNG, HP/phase, animations and telegraph data, locked aim and stable target, death flags, projectile snapshots, bounded recent damage IDs and event sequence.
- Three HP phases (36/24/12 boundaries) have increasingly fast windups and attack pressure: aim-locked fan shots, aimed quake/cover fracture and airborne dive/impact. Readable telegraph event precedes all attacks. Stage-renderable fields: `x/y/w/h`, `hp/maxHp`, `phase`, `mode`, `modeTimer`, `attack`, `aim`, `targetId`, `telegraph` with `remaining/duration/targetX/targetY/radius`, `projectiles`, `flash`, `invulnerability`, `deathTimer`, and `revealed`.
- Events (`id`, `tick`, `characterId`, `type`) include `devil-reveal`, `devil-telegraph`, `devil-shot`, `devil-cover-damage`, `devil-impact`, `devil-hit-player`, `devil-hit`, `devil-phase`, `devil-defeated` and `devil-death-complete`. Only the adapter may apply cover damage or player hits, and only extraction/award owners may summon the helicopter or unlock the precise devil.
- `damageEvents` accepts `{id,damage,targetId:'devil'}` (or matching `characterId`; omitted `targetId` is assumed pre-routed). Each source ID counts once in bounded recent-history memory; unique simultaneous hits aggregate to a capped 6 HP per vulnerability window. Boss hit iframes: 0.24s. A dead boss never fires/receives further damage; exactly one `devil-defeated` event is emitted. During a team wipe, attack clock freezes, live projectiles are cleared, and an interrupted warning is canceled; the death animation can still complete.
- Frame `dt` is seconds, capped at 0.1; nonfinite/zero/paused updates do not change state. At most 20 living projectiles, 32 ordinary events per frame, two targetable players, 32 incoming damage events and 160 recent hit IDs are retained. Cover collision expects `isSolid(tileCol,tileRow)` and 16px tiles. The update returns new state, not mutations to supplied player or boss objects.

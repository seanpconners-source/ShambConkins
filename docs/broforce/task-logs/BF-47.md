## Source-grounded behavior

The real mission adapter maps BF-11 `grunt → rifleman`, `gunner → turret`, `charger → melee`; authored `scout → rifleman`, `sentry → turret`, `heavy → carrier`. `mission.js` sends BF-15 enemy-shot events through BF-14 collision-tested pistol projectiles rather than directly damaging a selected target.

### Reproduced on pinned base

1. Enemy turret at (100,138), steel cover at tile (8,9), living P1 (158,146) hidden behind steel and living P2 (24,146) visible. P1 was closer, so the original nearest-first AI produced **zero alert/shot events** in 120 ticks even though P2 was visible.
2. Enemy turret first telegraphed `dir:+1` toward P1 at x=158. P1 crossed behind to x=24 during the warning. The original AI fired an `enemy-shot dir:+1` after crossing, while the mission targets the player's current position, so the actual projectile would redirect to the opposite side without an appropriate warning.

### Scoped corrections

- Target selection now selects the closest **living, visible** player. Real terrain LOS checks govern each candidate before choosing, preserving deterministic input-order ties.
- A committed attack warning cancels when its target crosses behind the telegraphed facing direction. A new warning must be issued for the opposite direction.
- New optional `activeBounds:{x,y,width,height}` in `advanceBroforceEnemies`: off-screen enemies preserve physics, AI remainder, grace, cooldown and RNG, while externally inflicted deaths still publish once. Missing/invalid bounds retain existing all-world activity. Per-call actor (24) and event (128) caps remain unchanged.

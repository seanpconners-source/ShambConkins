## Implementation

- Four procedurally synthesized, stage-specific signatures: jungle's low nocturnal pulses and band-passed life; factory's square-wave mechanical metronome and low machinery; city's detuned siren-like steps and high-passed rain; desert's sparse sine drones and band-passed wind. No sampled or downloaded sounds, DOM, network, or added AudioContext.
- Sound and music are independently gated by profile settings. `reducedMotion` attenuates all ambience. `setIntensity` clamps to 0..1, readjusts live level and can re-enable a previously fully muted environment.
- Lazy creation on host `start()` gesture; suspended shared AudioContext resumes on that gesture. Pause, stop, context-unlock cancellation and rapid stage changes disconnect owned nodes, terminate pending sources, cancel the single timeout, and never close the shared context.
- Original short grains (one lazy cached 0.5-second noise buffer), 12-voice cap, 1.2-second scheduling lookahead, 550ms self-clearing timeout; skipped beats after stalls; no `setInterval`.

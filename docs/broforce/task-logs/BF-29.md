## Implementation

- Jungle delegates exclusively to existing BF-24 `drawBroforceWorld`, retaining the canonical original canopy/outpost renderer; supplies an empty map for null-map safety.
- Factory paints independent parallax smokestacks, furnace bays, animated steam, gears, warning/indicator panels, background conveyors, pipes and sodium lamps.
- City Siege paints its own distant, medium and near apartment silhouettes, lit windows, roof HVAC, rooftop water/fire equipment, masonry wear, lamp posts and night street haze.
- Desert Assault paints a sunlit arid sky, three parallax mesa bands, facet-cut dunes, cacti/scrub, concrete fortified silhouettes and subdued animated heat haze.
- Existing collision terrain is rendered from the *current* `map.tiles` (row-major tile type IDs 0..5), with 5 biome-appropriate material appearances per new theme and correct culling; empty cells receive **no** platform-like solid overlay. Drawings and tiny edge highlights remain wholly inside occupied tiles. No changes to physics, terrain mutation, state, saves, assets or other workers' files.
- Optional `camera.zoom` is treated as screen pixels per world pixel, while viewport is screen canvas dimensions and camera x/y are world top-left. The renderer scopes clipping/transforms and restores context in `finally`; procedural detail uses stable hashed motifs, a bounded module-level motif cache and optional once-built OffscreenCanvas tile atlases. `quality:'low'` suppresses extra steam, fine masonry/window decorations, heat haze and city flecks.

## Exported APIs

- `createBroforceDirector({seed=1,difficulty=1}={})`: serializable director-local seed, RNG, integer tick, fractional remainder, phase, cooldown and bounded marker-use history; clamps difficulty to 0.5–4.
- `advanceBroforceDirector(state,dt,scene)`: pure state transition returning `{state,events}`; `dt` in seconds with a six-step / 0.1s frame limit. `scene` requires the assigned `players`, `camera`, `world`, `activeEnemies` and `spawnMarkers` shapes. Additional optional flags: `scene.paused`, `scene.disabled`, `scene.enabled===false`, `scene.completed`, `scene.bossZone`, `scene.bossActive`, and `scene.phase` (`paused` / `disabled` / `boss` / `completed`).
- Spawn output is `spawn-enemy` with unique encounter-local `spawnId`, `enemyType` (`grunt`, `gunner`, `charger`), `x`, `y`. The adapter handles enemy creation, lifecycle, boss and completion transitions; director never grants rewards or mutates the host game.

## Mechanics and safeguards

- Fixed 1/60-second simulation; independent serializable RNG; variable cooldown based on stage time, camera progress, difficulty and nearby living player count; short breathers and five-second opening calm.
- Spawn only ahead of and outside the current camera; also reject near players, the first 240–480 world pixels, out-of-world markers, unsafe vertical positions, and malformed marker coordinates.
- At most two spawns per marker, at least six seconds between reuses, finite live-enemy budget, at most 56 total mission spawns, one event per active spawn attempt, at most 1024 marker candidates scanned. Marker data and director state are never mutated by the caller-facing APIs.
- No random spawning from 78% stage progress; permanent boss lockdown at 92% or an explicit boss flag. Pause/disable freezes ticks, completion terminates director pacing.

## Interface and behavior

- `createBroforceEnemy({id,type,x,y,seed=1}={})`: deterministic plain enemy state. Canonical types: `rifleman`, `melee`, `turret`, `carrier` (named aliases accepted). Caller should supply unique stable IDs and place enemies on valid stage tiles; this module does not spawn them.
- `advanceBroforceEnemies(enemies,{dt,players,isSolid,worldBounds,rand,paused=false})`: returns `{enemies,events}` without mutating inputs. Actor coordinates are **top-left pixels**; player target coordinates are interpreted as target points in world pixels. `isSolid(col,row)` takes integer **16-pixel tile** indices. Bounds use `{minX,minY,maxX,maxY}` or `{width,height}` in world pixels.
- The fixed 60-Hz AI steps rely on existing `src/engines/broforce/physics.js` for 120-Hz gravity/collision. `dt` is capped at 0.1 seconds/call; pause freezes state/events; up to 24 distinct enemies; no new render, spawn, damage, terrain, save, roster or character-selection logic.
- Events have stable per-enemy `eventId`, `enemyId`: `alert`, `attack-tell`, `enemy-shot`, `contact-hit`, `enemy-explosion`, `enemy-hit`, `enemy-down`. Aim warnings and spawn grace precede attacks; proximity hit and blast occur once per committed attack. Death is terminal and emitted once, including external `hp=0`/`state='dead'` changes. The combat adapter owns application of effects/damage and all projectiles, not this AI.
- Riflemen patrol, alert and retreat from nearby fighters; melee and explosive carriers aggressively close gaps; turrets aim and fire while horizontally fixed. Tiles occlude sight, actors check walls/edges, hit stuns cancel attacks, and two players retain distinct IDs. RNG is injected or per-enemy seeded, never global mutable randomness.

# Arcade group art to make (Nov 12): one icon per group

The Arcade's top shelf is now groups (All games): Console Classics, Arcade Classics, Board Games, Mobile Hits, Sports,
Paddy's Pub, Party Games, Game Shows & Trivia, Movie Night, Casino Night, Pinball Tables, Boss Select. Paddy's, Casino,
Pinball and Bosses already have their own art. The 8 below show a stand-in mosaic of four of their games until they
get their own icon.

Same rules as `START-HERE.md`. Paste the style block first, then one prompt per chat. **File name = the slot name.**

> Square app icon for a party game app's game group, polished glossy painted video-game style, bold shapes that read at
> 64 px, centred subject with a little breathing room, rich colour, soft glow. Our own designs: no real company logos,
> no copied characters, no screenshots; references to games and shows are fine as themes. No words or letters unless a
> logo is asked for.

Sizes: **icon** 512x512 (square, clear background or flat #00FF00) · **logo** transparent, about 1100 px wide, letters
only plus a small emblem (optional) · **background** 1600x900 landscape (optional; every background goes through the
Background Picker before it ships).

Look at the reference icons in `examples/` (Pinball, Casino, Mario Kart): match their finish, size of subject and glow.

## Hand-back (for Claude)

Owner uploads to `art-inbox/arcade/` (or attaches in chat). Import with `python3 tools/import-arcade-art.py <folder>`
(icons 512 → `assets/tiles/<slot>.webp`, logos trimmed → `assets/keyart/<slot>-logo.webp`, backgrounds 1600 lossless →
`assets/keyart/<slot>.webp` + a KEY_BOX each, then the Background Picker). The mosaic goes away by itself once
`assets/tiles/<slot>` exists.

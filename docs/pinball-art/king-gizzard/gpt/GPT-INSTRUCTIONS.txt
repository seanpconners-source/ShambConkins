WHAT YOU'RE MAKING
Six pictures for the King Gizzard table: the playfield (the big one), the backglass, the apron, the logo, the
Lizard Wizard and the pop-bumper cap. Each one below has: the file(s) to upload, a prompt to paste, what to check
in the result, and what to type if it's wrong. GPT doesn't need any measurements: the uploaded sketches show it the
layout. Claude does the exact fitting afterwards (sizes, transparency, darkening under the lights).

Look at how-the-table-looks-now.png first: it's the whole table with every part the game draws on top of the art
(the upper "Rats' Nest" playfield with its two mini flippers, the bumpers, the yellow ramp, the snake, the lights).
The game draws all of those itself, so the pictures must NOT contain them.

THE TWO MISTAKES GPT MAKES (seen on the Black Sabbath table)
- It paints things the game already draws, so they show up twice. Here that's THE SNAKE: the game draws an
  animated rattlesnake up the right side. Never let GPT paint a snake on the playfield.
- It puts the title wherever it likes. The title must sit in the yellow box of the sketch, not higher.

Use a NEW ChatGPT chat for each picture. Download each result at full size.

  The attached image is ONLY a layout sketch: follow where each area is, but do not draw any of its lines, boxes,
  labels, words or flat colours. Paint a tall portrait picture (about 1 wide by 2 tall, for example 1024 x 2048)
  in a 1970s psychedelic gig-poster style: rich airbrushed colours, deep night purple with violet, acid green,
  tangerine and hot pink, a little film grain. Follow the sketch area by area:
  - The rounded top, inside the dark curved band, is a separate little round playfield: the green, cratered
    surface of a small alien planet, with a few cartoon rats scurrying on it. Give it a slightly raised rim and a
    soft shadow along its bottom edge.
  - The dark curved band around it and the dark strips down both sides are racing lanes: keep them plain and
    very dark (deep purple-black), with no objects in them.
  - The grey band on the right marks where an animated snake will be added later. Keep that strip plain and
    medium-dark there (just the background), and do NOT paint any snake, serpent or worm anywhere in the picture.
  - Under the planet, behind the three circles: melting concentric rings glowing softly, nothing important (round
    bumpers will sit on top).
  - The middle: a Gila monster lizard crawling left to right where the sketch says, and below it a big glowing
    nine-sided NONAGON emblem with melting psychedelic rings radiating out from it across the whole table.
  - Inside the yellow box: the words KING GIZZARD (big) and & THE LIZARD WIZARD (smaller, underneath) in bubbly,
    melting 1970s psychedelic lettering, tangerine to hot pink with a dark outline, filling the box, no higher.
  - Inside the green box: a lizard wizard standing full length, facing us: green lizard head with a yellow eye,
    a tall starry violet pointed hat, a violet robe with gold stars, a wooden staff with a glowing green orb.
  - The black bottom area is hidden; just continue the background there.
  Flat top-down view like real pinball playfield art, no perspective. No pinball parts: no flippers, bumpers,
  ramps, balls, lights or holes. No other text anywhere. No frame or border.

Check (and what to type if it's wrong):
- No snake anywhere: "Remove the snake completely, keep everything else the same."
- The title is inside the box area (just above the wizard), not near the top: "Move the KING GIZZARD title down
  so it sits just above the wizard, where the yellow box was in my sketch."
- No sketch words, boxes or outlines copied in: "Remove all the labels and box outlines from my sketch."
- The planet is round at the top and the side lanes are dark and empty.
- It's tall (about twice as tall as wide): "Make it a taller portrait, 1 by 2, by extending the bottom."
- No pinball parts painted in: "Remove the flippers / bumpers / balls."

KING GIZZARD & THE LIZARD WIZARD PINBALL - CUSTOM IMAGE SPECS
=============================================================

The table has 6 replaceable pictures (no toy: the Rattlesnake is animated by code). Each one has a template PNG in
this folder that shows the current art at the exact size and framing the game uses. Make a new picture the same size,
follow the rules below, save it with the exact file name, and put it in:   assets/pinball/king-gizzard/
then rebuild the game. Any picture you don't supply keeps the built-in art.
Each section ends with a PROMPT you can paste into an image tool (upload the matching template with it).

GENERAL RULES (all pictures)
- File names are lowercase and exact: playfield, backglass, apron, logo, character, bumper.
  Extension: .png, .webp or .jpg (.svg also works).
- Pictures that need a TRANSPARENT background (logo, character) must be PNG or WebP with a real alpha channel.
  A "checkerboard" painted into the image is NOT transparency; it will show up as a checkerboard. JPG has none.
- Transparent pixels show whatever is underneath (the playfield). Semi-transparent pixels blend with it.
- Use the exact pixel sizes below. Other sizes still load, but the picture gets stretched or cropped.
- Theme: King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard's "Gizzverse": a 1970s psychedelic gig poster. Melting concentric
  rings, nonagons (nine-sided shapes), a lizard wizard in a starry pointed hat with a glowing staff, a rattlesnake,
  rats escaping to a green planet (Planet B / Venus), a Gila monster, a flying banana, a dragon, fish. Colours:
  deep night purple, violet, acid green, tangerine, hot pink, a little turquoise. Darker art lets the lights stand out.
- Inspired-by only: don't copy real album covers or the band's real logo lettering, and no song lyrics.
- No text that must be read at small sizes: on a phone the whole table is about 390 pixels wide.
- Keep each file under about 2 MB (the game embeds them). Shrink with:  python3 tools/optimize-art.py

Coordinates below are in pixels of that picture, from its top-left corner (x to the right, y down).

2) backglass.png  -  1200 x 1600 px recommended (3:4 portrait; any size works), OPAQUE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the big painted "box art" shown as the intro card before a game, like the lit glass on top of a real
pinball machine.
How it is fitted:
- Intro card: the whole picture is shown, shrunk to fit the screen ("contain"); empty space around it is black.
  On a phone held upright the screen area is roughly 390 x 700, so a taller picture (e.g. 1080 x 1920) fills
  more of it; 1200 x 1600 leaves black bars above and below.
- Landscape screens only: a thin banner to the right of the score display uses the picture too, cropped to fill
  ("cover") a very wide short strip (about 4.5:1), taken from the MIDDLE of the picture.
So: put the main subject (the Lizard Wizard, the rattlesnake, the title) near the centre, and make the middle
horizontal band look good on its own. A title at the top is fine on the intro card.
PROMPT: Vintage 1970s pinball backglass painting, portrait 3:4, King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard themed: a lizard
wizard in a starry violet robe and pointed hat raising a glowing green staff, a huge rattlesnake coiled round his
feet, a spinning golden nonagon halo behind him, melting psychedelic rings in violet, tangerine, acid green and hot
pink, a small green planet with rats flying toward it, and a title across the top reading KING GIZZARD & THE
LIZARD WIZARD in bubbly melting psychedelic lettering. Rich airbrushed colours, high detail.

Readable text: the current cards are about 112 x 40 px in the band near y 260-360. Your picture replaces the

4) logo.png  -  1056 x 188 px (as the template), TRANSPARENT background REQUIRED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the KING GIZZARD & THE LIZARD WIZARD title on the playfield, also shown in the score banner (landscape).
How it is fitted: always shown whole, never cropped; aspect ratio kept.
- Playfield: drawn 528 px wide (in playfield.png pixels), centred at (472,1424). Its height follows its shape.
- Score banner (landscape only): shrunk to fit the space left of the score display.
Size rule: any width (about 1000+ px is sharp enough); keep the height at most 25% of the width (e.g. 1056 x 264).
Use a full-bleed design: the logo should reach the left and right edges with only a small margin.
Not shown on the playfield when a custom playfield.png is used (it still shows in the score banner).
PROMPT: The words KING GIZZARD (big) and & THE LIZARD WIZARD (smaller, underneath) as a pinball logo: bubbly,
melting 1970s psychedelic lettering in a tangerine-to-hot-pink gradient with a dark outline and an acid-green
glow, wide horizontal layout filling the image edge to edge, transparent background.

6) bumper.png  -  256 x 256 px (exact square), OPAQUE inside the circle
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the top of the pop bumpers (the three MICROTONAL bumpers under the Nest). One picture for all three.
How it is fitted: cut to the circle that touches all four edges (centre 128,128, radius 128). Corners outside the

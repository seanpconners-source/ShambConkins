BUCKETHEADLAND PLAYFIELD: MAKING BUCKETHEAD FIT THE TABLE
==========================================================

WHAT'S WRONG NOW (see mockup-now-vs-target.png, left side)
- Buckethead is painted too big and too low. The 2X-6X lights sit on his bucket, his guitar runs under the right
  slingshot and its headstock pokes into the right lane, and the guitar body sits under the left flipper.
- The BUCKETHEADLAND sign spans the full width, so its first and last letters are hidden under the Roller Coaster ramp
  (left) and the Robot ramp (right). The 2X-6X lights cover its bottom edge.
- Because he is painted into the playfield, he can't be moved. (The separate Buckethead picture, character.webp, is not
  shown when a custom playfield is used.)

THE FIX (mockup-now-vs-target.png, right side)
- The sign gets smaller and sits between the two ramps, under the JORDAN star and above the 2X-6X row.
- Buckethead gets smaller and moves up between the slingshots, under the 2X-6X row, centred on the table's centre line,
  with the guitar kept inside the slingshots.
- To do that, the playfield needs a version of its bottom half WITHOUT Buckethead and WITHOUT the sign. GPT makes that
  (step 1). Claude then places your existing sign (logo.webp) and Buckethead (character.webp) on it in exactly the right
  spots, blends it into the top of the playfield and checks it in the game. You don't need to give GPT any measurements.
  The background in the mock-up is just a blur standing in for the GPT picture.


STEP 1 (needed): the empty stage
--------------------------------
New ChatGPT chat. Upload: upload-1-current-stage.png
Paste this prompt exactly:

  I'm making the playfield art for a pinball table. The attached image is the bottom section of my current
  playfield: "Bucketheadland", a spooky night-time theme park and circus. Recreate this exact same scene in the same
  painted style, with the same colours, lighting and point of view, but completely REMOVE the guitarist in the
  bucket hat and completely REMOVE the BUCKETHEADLAND sign. Where they were, continue the scene naturally: the red
  and cream striped circus big-top canvas fanning out from the centre, glowing marquee bulbs along the tent edges,
  small tents at the sides, and low red and purple smoke rolling across the bottom. Keep the top edge of the picture
  (the tents and bulbs) as close to the original as possible so it blends back in. Keep the middle of the picture
  calm and medium-dark, like an empty stage glowing softly dark red, because a character and a sign will be placed
  on top of it later. No people, no characters, no faces, no text, no letters, no logos, no signs, no frame or
  border. Square image, 1024 x 1024.

Check the result before sending it to Claude:
- No person, mask, guitar or hand left anywhere. If there is, reply: "Remove every person and the guitar, keep
  everything else the same."
- No letters or words anywhere (GPT likes to sneak a sign back in). If there are, reply: "Remove all text and signs."
- The centre should be fairly plain. Bright bulbs or stars in the middle would compete with the table's lights.
- If the colours drift a lot from the original, reply: "Match the colours and lighting of the attached image more
  closely."

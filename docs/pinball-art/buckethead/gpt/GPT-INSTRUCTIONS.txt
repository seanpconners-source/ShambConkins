BUCKETHEADLAND PLAYFIELD: BUCKETHEAD PLAYS THE LEFT FLIPPER
===========================================================

THE IDEA (see target-layout.png)
Buckethead stands between the slingshots playing his guitar, and the LEFT FLIPPER IS HIS STRUMMING HAND. His arm is
painted on the playfield and ends at the wrist right on the flipper's pivot. The hand itself is a separate picture that
the game draws on the left flipper, so it moves: at rest it hangs just below the strings, and every flip swings it up
across the strings like a strum. The game already supports a picture on each bottom flipper (new slots
flipper-left / flipper-right). The right flipper keeps its normal look.

WHAT ELSE GETS FIXED
- Now the 2X-6X lights sit on his bucket: the bucket top moves just under that row of lights.
- Now his guitar headstock pokes into the right lane: the guitar is held more upright so the headstock stays inside the
  right slingshot.
- Now the BUCKETHEADLAND sign's first and last letters are hidden under the two ramps: Claude places your existing
  sign (logo.webp) smaller, between the ramps, above the 2X-6X row.

HOW IT'S MADE: three GPT pictures, then Claude assembles them
1. The empty stage: the bottom half of your current playfield with Buckethead and the sign removed.
2. Buckethead in the new pose, on a transparent background.
3. His hand holding a pick, on a transparent background.
Claude then cuts the painted hand off picture 2 at the wrist, places him so the wrist sits exactly on the flipper
pivot, blends everything into the playfield, puts the sign back, fits the hand to the flipper, and checks it in the
game with the flipper down and up. GPT doesn't need any measurements; the sketches show it the layout.

Use a NEW ChatGPT chat for each picture. Download each result at full size.

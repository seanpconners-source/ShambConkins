BUCKETHEADLAND PINBALL - CUSTOM IMAGE SPECS
============================================

The table has 9 replaceable pictures. Each one has a template PNG in this folder that shows the current art at the
exact size and framing the game uses. Make a new picture the same size, follow the rules below, save it with the
exact file name, and put it in:   assets/pinball/buckethead/
then rebuild the game. Any picture you don't supply keeps the built-in art.

GENERAL RULES (all pictures)
- File names are lowercase and exact: playfield, backglass, apron, logo, character, bumper, toy.
  Extension: .png, .webp or .jpg (.svg also works).
- Pictures that need a TRANSPARENT background (logo, character, and ideally toy) must be PNG or WebP with a real
  alpha channel. A "checkerboard" painted into the image is NOT transparency; it will show up as a checkerboard.
  JPG has no transparency at all.
- Transparent pixels show whatever is underneath (the playfield). Semi-transparent pixels blend with it.
- Use the exact pixel sizes below. Other sizes still load, but the picture gets stretched or cropped (see "How it
  is fitted" for each one).
- Theme: a night-time theme park (Buckethead's "Bucketheadland"): dark purple/maroon sky, lit rides, Buckethead
  (white blank mask, red-and-white striped KFC-style bucket, white guitar). Darker art lets the table's lights
  stand out.
- No text that must be read at small sizes: on a phone the whole table is about 390 pixels wide.
- Keep each file under about 2 MB (the game embeds them). Shrink with:  python3 tools/optimize-art.py

Coordinates below are in pixels of that picture, from its top-left corner (x to the right, y down).


1) playfield.png  -  1028 x 2134 px (exact), OPAQUE (no transparency needed)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the painted wood of the whole table, under every ball, light, ramp and bumper.
How it is fitted: stretched over the whole table (1028 x 2134 = 2 px per mm). Not cropped.
Replaces EVERYTHING painted on the playfield now: sky, Ferris wheel, signs, the BUCKETHEADLAND logo, Buckethead
and the "WELCOME TO THE PARK" text. If you use a custom playfield, logo.png and character.png no longer appear on the
playfield, so paint the logo and Buckethead into this picture yourself.
What is hidden / covered:
- Outside the cabinet shape (cut off): left of x=24, right of x=1000, and the two top corners outside the arch. The
  top is a half-circle, centre (512,512), radius 488 (its top touches y=24).
- The shooter lane on the right (x 920-1000, from y 600 down) is darkened.
- Bottom: the apron covers everything below a line from (24,1760) to (220,1952) to (336,2008) to (608,2008) to
  (724,1952) to (920,1760). Nothing below that line is seen.
- The game also darkens the edges a little (vignette) and adds a faint gloss on top.
- The table's centre line is x = 472 (not 514): the shooter lane takes the right-hand strip.
Things the game draws on top (keep important art away from them; see playfield-parts.png and playfield-guide.png):
- Top lanes B-O-T: x 360-600, y 160-240.
- Three pop bumpers (round caps with a metal ring): centres (400,424), (564,424), (482,556), about 116 px across.
- Giant Robot toy: about x 712-904, y 300-512.
- Roller Coaster ramp: see-through red plastic down the left side (your art shows through, tinted red).
- Robot Ramp and Coop wireforms: thin chrome rails on the right/centre (see-through).
- Chicken Coop hole at (472,872), the EGG targets just below it at y 948, the Golden Egg lane at about x 640-700,
  y 640-960, the Haunted Ride hole at (836,1228).
- Coloured round/arrow light inserts (45 of them) are see-through tinted lenses: your art shows under them.
  The big ones: JORDAN star at (472,1240), COASTER arrow (300,1232), ROBOT arrow (744,1232), 2X-6X row at y 1500,
  SHOOT AGAIN at (472,1810).
- Slingshots: x 188-264 and 680-756, y 1524-1736. Flippers at about y 1890.
Good spots for big art: the band y 1290-1450 across the middle (where the logo is now), the area y 1500-1800
between the slingshots (where Buckethead is now), and the sky behind the bumpers.
Use playfield-parts.png as the top layer in an image editor to check alignment.


2) backglass.png  -  1200 x 1600 px recommended (3:4 portrait; any size works), OPAQUE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the big painted "box art" shown as the intro card before a game, like the lit glass on top of a real
pinball machine.
How it is fitted:
- Intro card: the whole picture is shown, shrunk to fit the screen ("contain"); empty space around it is black.
  On a phone held upright the screen area is roughly 390 x 700, so a taller picture (e.g. 1080 x 1920) fills
  more of it; 1200 x 1600 leaves black bars above and below.
- Landscape screens only: a thin banner to the right of the score display uses the picture too, cropped to fill
  ("cover") a very wide short strip (about 4.5:1), taken from the MIDDLE of the picture.
So: put the main subject (Buckethead, title) near the centre, and make the middle horizontal band look good on its
own. A title at the top is fine on the intro card.


3) apron.png  -  896 x 374 px (exact), OPAQUE
---------------------------------------------
What it is: the metal panel at the very bottom of the table, around and below the flippers (where balls drain).
Real tables print the rules cards and the logo here.
How it is fitted: scaled to cover a 448 x 187 mm box ("cover"; with this exact size nothing is cropped), then cut to
the apron shape. A thin metal edge is drawn around it.
Visible part only: everything BELOW the line from (0,0) to (196,192) to (312,248) to (584,248) to (700,192) to
(896,0). The V-shaped area above that line (top middle and top corners) is never seen; the flippers sit there.
So the usable area is two slanted wings at the left and right, and a full-width band from y 248 to 374.
Readable text: the current cards are about 112 x 40 px in the band near y 260-360.
Transparency: not needed; transparent pixels there would show the dark table colour.


4) logo.png  -  1092 x 250 px (as the template), TRANSPARENT background REQUIRED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the BUCKETHEADLAND title painted on the playfield, also shown in the score banner (landscape).
How it is fitted: always shown whole, never cropped; aspect ratio kept.
- Playfield: drawn 546 px wide (in playfield.png pixels), centred at (472,1400). Its height follows its shape.
- Score banner (landscape only): shrunk to fit the space left of the score display.
Size rule: any width (about 1000+ px is sharp enough); keep the height at most 30% of the width (e.g. 1092 x 330
at most). Taller logos run into the JORDAN star above and the 2X-6X lights below.
Use a full-bleed design: the logo should reach the left and right edges of the image with only a small margin,
because the image width is what sets the drawn width. Extra empty space on the sides makes the logo smaller.
Not shown on the playfield when a custom playfield.png is used (it still shows in the score banner).


5) character.png  -  660 x 805 px (as the template), TRANSPARENT background REQUIRED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the hero art (Buckethead) on the playfield between the two slingshots, above the flippers.
How it is fitted: always shown whole, aspect ratio kept. Drawn 220 px wide (in playfield pixels) centred on the
table's centre line; the point 40% of the way down the image sits at y 1640.
Size rule: keep 660 wide and a height between 660 and 805 (taller runs into the 2X-6X lights above and the
SHOOT AGAIN light below). Keep the figure inside the frame: anything outside the image is lost. (The built-in
Buckethead's guitar is wider than this frame, which is why the guitar's head is cut off in the template.)
The playfield's "WELCOME TO THE PARK" text is painted over the lower part of this picture.
Not shown when a custom playfield.png is used.


6) bumper.png  -  256 x 256 px (exact square), OPAQUE inside the circle
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the top of the pop bumpers (the three round mushroom bumpers that kick the ball). One picture is used
for all three.
How it is fitted: cut to the circle that touches all four edges (centre 128,128, radius 128). Corners outside the
circle are never seen, so transparency there doesn't matter. Each cap is only about 45 mm across, about 35
screen pixels on a phone, so use a bold, simple design (big shapes, at most 2-3 letters).
When a bumper is hit the game flashes it brighter (a white wash) and shrinks it slightly; the metal ring around it
is drawn by the game.


7) toy.png  -  400 x 350 px (exact, 8:7), TRANSPARENT background recommended, but FILL THE FRAME
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the Giant Robot, the moving toy at the upper right. It walks left and right during the robot battle.
How it is fitted: stretched to a 96 x 84 mm box (no cropping; a different shape gets squashed, so keep 8:7).
The game draws the shadow and the white hit flash in the picture's own outline, so a transparent background is fine.
The ball bounces off a house-shaped outline about 14 mm taller than the box (the roof peak sits above the box top),
so a pointed head/antenna at the top centre matches what the ball hits.
The picture is not animated (no walking legs, blinking eyes or glowing beam like the built-in robot).



8) flipper-left.png / flipper-right.png  -  600 x 200 px (exact, 3:1), TRANSPARENT background REQUIRED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: a picture on a bottom flipper that swings with it (e.g. Buckethead's strumming hand on the left flipper).
How it is fitted: drawn in the flipper's own frame. Left flipper: the pivot (where it turns) is at (120,100) and the
end of the flipper is at (510,100), so draw it pointing right with the pivot end on the left. Right flipper: the
mirror image (pivot at (480,100), end at (90,100)). The real flipper is a tapered bar: a 126 px circle round the pivot
narrowing to a 68 px round end. Keep the art close to that shape: the ball bounces off the real flipper, not the
picture. The picture is drawn under the ball, with a soft shadow in its outline. Either slot can be left out (that
flipper keeps its painted look). Templates: flipper-left.png and flipper-right.png show the painted flippers in this
frame.


REFERENCE FILES (not used by the game)
- playfield-guide.png: the whole table as it looks now, with every part.
- playfield-parts.png: only what the game draws on top of a playfield picture, transparent everywhere else.
  Lay it over your playfield to check nothing important is hidden.

NOT REPLACEABLE BY PICTURES (drawn by code): the score display (DMD), the upper flipper, slingshots, ramps and rails, light
inserts, drop targets and the ball.

Remake the templates: node tools/pinball-art-templates.mjs

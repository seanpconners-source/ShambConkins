BLACK SABBATH PINBALL - CUSTOM IMAGE SPECS
==========================================

The table has 7 replaceable pictures. Each one has a template PNG in this folder that shows the current art at the
exact size and framing the game uses. Make a new picture the same size, follow the rules below, save it with the
exact file name, and put it in:   assets/pinball/black-sabbath/
then rebuild the game. Any picture you don't supply keeps the built-in art.
Each section ends with a PROMPT you can paste into an image tool (upload the matching template with it).

GENERAL RULES (all pictures)
- File names are lowercase and exact: playfield, backglass, apron, logo, character, bumper, toy.
  Extension: .png, .webp or .jpg (.svg also works).
- Pictures that need a TRANSPARENT background (logo, character, and ideally toy) must be PNG or WebP with a real
  alpha channel. A "checkerboard" painted into the image is NOT transparency; it will show up as a checkerboard.
  JPG has no transparency at all.
- Transparent pixels show whatever is underneath (the playfield). Semi-transparent pixels blend with it.
- Use the exact pixel sizes below. Other sizes still load, but the picture gets stretched or cropped (see "How it
  is fitted" for each one).
- Theme: Black Sabbath, gothic 1970s heavy metal: night, a full moon, bare trees, an old water mill with a cloaked
  hooded figure, a misty graveyard, bats, war pigs in army helmets, a hulking iron man, Tony Iommi's black
  twin-horned guitar. Colours: near-black, deep purple, violet glow, blood red, sickly grave green, bone white.
  Darker art lets the table's lights stand out.
- Inspired-by only: don't copy the real album covers or the band's real logo, and no song lyrics.
- No text that must be read at small sizes: on a phone the whole table is about 390 pixels wide.
- Keep each file under about 2 MB (the game embeds them). Shrink with:  python3 tools/optimize-art.py

Coordinates below are in pixels of that picture, from its top-left corner (x to the right, y down).

  (724,1952) to (920,1760). Nothing below that line is seen.

2) backglass.png  -  1200 x 1600 px recommended (3:4 portrait; any size works), OPAQUE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the big painted "box art" shown as the intro card before a game, like the lit glass on top of a real
pinball machine.
How it is fitted:
- Intro card: the whole picture is shown, shrunk to fit the screen ("contain"); empty space around it is black.
  On a phone held upright the screen area is roughly 390 x 700, so a taller picture (e.g. 1080 x 1920) fills
  more of it; 1200 x 1600 leaves black bars above and below.
- Landscape screens only: a thin banner to the right of the score display uses the picture too, cropped to fill
  ("cover") a very wide short strip (about 4.5:1), taken from the MIDDLE of the picture.
So: put the main subject (Iron Man, the cloaked figure, the title) near the centre, and make the middle horizontal
band look good on its own. A title at the top is fine on the intro card.
PROMPT: Vintage 1970s pinball backglass painting, portrait 3:4, Black Sabbath themed: a full moon over a gothic
water mill at night, a cloaked hooded figure in the foreground, a towering riveted iron giant with a glowing red
visor on the left, a row of pig soldiers in army helmets marching along the bottom, bats and lightning in a violet
sky, and a marquee across the top reading BLACK SABBATH in heavy gothic serif lettering with dripping red edges.
Rich airbrushed colours, dramatic lighting, high detail.


3) apron.png  -  896 x 374 px (exact), OPAQUE
---------------------------------------------
What it is: the metal panel at the very bottom of the table, around and below the flippers (where balls drain).
Real tables print the rules cards and the logo here.
How it is fitted: scaled to cover a 448 x 187 mm box ("cover"; with this exact size nothing is cropped), then cut to
the apron shape. A thin edge is drawn around it.
Visible part only: everything BELOW the line from (0,0) to (196,192) to (312,248) to (584,248) to (700,192) to
(896,0). The V-shaped area above that line (top middle and top corners) is never seen; the flippers sit there.
So the usable area is two slanted wings at the left and right, and a full-width band from y 248 to 374.
Readable text: the current cards are about 112 x 40 px in the band near y 260-360. Your picture replaces the
painted cards, so include any text you want.
Transparency: not needed; transparent pixels there would show the dark table colour.
PROMPT: Pinball apron artwork, wide panel (2.4:1), dark gunmetal with purple trim, flat front-on view: two cream
instruction cards with purple headers, left "BLACK SABBATH / 3 BALLS PER GAME / BURY 3 FOR WAR PIGS", right
"SPELL M-A-S-T-E-R / RAISES THE HAND OF DOOM / EVERY 2ND: EXTRA BALL", and a centre plate reading HAND OF DOOM.
Keep the cards in the bottom third; the top middle is hidden behind the flippers.


4) logo.png  -  1152 x 282 px (as the template), TRANSPARENT background REQUIRED
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the BLACK SABBATH title painted on the playfield, also shown in the score banner (landscape).
How it is fitted: always shown whole, never cropped; aspect ratio kept.
- Playfield: drawn 576 px wide (in playfield.png pixels), centred at (472,1384). Its height follows its shape.
- Score banner (landscape only): shrunk to fit the space left of the score display.
Size rule: any width (about 1000+ px is sharp enough); keep the height at most 25% of the width (e.g. 1152 x 288
at most). Taller logos run into the WAR PIGS star above and the 2X-6X lights below.
Use a full-bleed design: the logo should reach the left and right edges of the image with only a small margin,
because the image width is what sets the drawn width. (The built-in logo doesn't fill the template's width; a
full-width picture will show bigger than it does.)
Not shown on the playfield when a custom playfield.png is used (it still shows in the score banner).
PROMPT: The words BLACK SABBATH as a pinball playfield logo: heavy gothic serif lettering, bone white with a dark
outline, a violet glow and a few blood-red drips, wide horizontal layout filling the image edge to edge,
transparent background.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the hero art (Tony Iommi's black twin-horned guitar) on the playfield between the two slingshots,

How it is fitted: always shown whole, aspect ratio kept. Drawn 220 px wide (in playfield pixels) centred on the

Size rule: keep 660 wide and a height between 660 and 896. Keep the bottom quarter of the image mostly empty: the
"BIRMINGHAM · 1970" caption, the SHOOT AGAIN light and the HAND OF DOOM light sit just below. Keep the subject
inside the frame: anything outside the image is lost.
Not shown when a custom playfield.png is used.
PROMPT: A black twin-horned electric guitar (SG style) with a white pickguard, lying diagonally, seen from above,
painted in a dark gothic style with a violet rim light, the whole guitar inside the top three quarters of the
image, transparent background.


6) bumper.png  -  256 x 256 px (exact square), OPAQUE inside the circle
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: the top of the pop bumpers (the three round WAR PIGS bumpers that kick the ball). One picture is used
for all three.
How it is fitted: cut to the circle that touches all four edges (centre 128,128, radius 128). Corners outside the
circle are never seen, so transparency there doesn't matter. Each cap is only about 45 mm across, about 35
screen pixels on a phone, so use a bold, simple design (big shapes, at most 2-3 letters).
When a bumper is hit the game flashes it brighter (a pink-white wash) and shrinks it slightly; the metal ring
around it is drawn by the game.
PROMPT: Round pop-bumper cap seen straight from above: a pink pig face wearing a steel army helmet, bold cartoon
war-poster style, big simple shapes, centred in a circle that fills the square image.


7) toy.png  -  336 x 464 px (exact, 0.72:1 portrait), TRANSPARENT background recommended, but FILL THE FRAME
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it is: IRON MAN, the moving toy at the upper left. He steps toward the flippers during his mode.
How it is fitted: stretched to an 84 x 116 mm box (no cropping; a different shape gets squashed, so keep 0.72:1).
Important: the game draws a dark rectangle the size of the whole box as its shadow (offset down-right), and the
hit flash is also a white wash over the whole box. With a transparent background, that shadow and flash show as
rectangles around him. So make Iron Man fill nearly the whole image (a hulking, boxy figure works best), or paint a
background plate behind him.
The ball bounces off a house-shaped outline: a pointed head at the top centre (at the top edge of the box), and an
underside that slopes from about y 444 on the left to about y 372 on the right (in toy.png pixels), so rebounds fall
away to the right. His legs below that line are only for looks.
The picture is not animated (no stepping legs or blazing visor like the built-in Iron Man).
PROMPT: A hulking riveted iron man robot from the 1970s, front view, full body, dark gunmetal plates with rivets,
a glowing red visor slit in a pointed helmet, heavy fists, standing straight, filling the whole frame, painted toy
style, transparent background.

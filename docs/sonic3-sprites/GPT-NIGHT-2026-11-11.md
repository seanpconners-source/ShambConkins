## 1. Walk cycles (MOST IMPORTANT): `walk-p1.png` and `walk-p2.png`

**Why:** the current walk rows are mostly "feet together" frames with only one or two real steps, so walking looks like
shuffling. The game uses the run frames for now (leaned back), but a slow walk still shuffles.

**One picture per runner**, one row, left to right, evenly spaced on flat green:

1. **Frame 0 = the standing pose copied from the current sheet** (the importer uses it to match the scale; it is not used).
2. **Then 8 walk poses**, facing RIGHT, a full stride cycle that loops (frame 8 leads back into frame 1):

| # | Pose | Legs | Arms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contact (right foot forward) | right heel touching down well in front, left toe pushing off behind: the widest stance | left arm forward, right arm back |
| 2 | down | weight on the right leg, knee bent, body ~2 px lower; left foot lifting behind | swinging back toward the sides |
| 3 | passing | right leg straight under the body, left knee lifted passing it | arms by the sides |
| 4 | up | right leg pushing off on the toe, body ~2 px higher; left leg reaching forward | right arm starting forward |
| 5 | contact (left foot forward) | mirror of 1: left heel down in front, right toe behind | right arm forward, left arm back |
| 6 | down | mirror of 2 | mirror |
| 7 | passing | mirror of 3 | mirror |
| 8 | up | mirror of 4 | mirror |

- The feet must **clearly separate**: in poses 1 and 5 the gap between the heels is about the body's width; in 3 and 7
  one knee is clearly lifted. Big shoes, big readable steps (Mega Drive walk cycles exaggerate this).
- **Same size as the standing pose**: same shoe size, same body size. The neck opening (where the face goes) stays at
  the same height as standing, give or take the 2 px bob. Feet on one shared ground line.
- Body upright (a slight forward lean is fine; the RUN row already leans hard).
- Hedgehog (`walk-p1`): blue-purple body, purple arms, white gloves, red-and-white sneakers, scarf, NO quills (the game
  draws the quills behind the face). Fox (`walk-p2`): red-orange body, cream chest, teal sneakers, the three tails
  behind, swaying a little opposite to the hips.

> frames where he faces away).

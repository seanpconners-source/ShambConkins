# Seanic: art fixes and additions (for ChatGPT), Nov 11

The game already works around each of these. A new picture just makes it look right. Same rules as the other briefs:
our own characters, Mega Drive Sonic style (bold dark outlines, bright colours), no words, transparent background (or flat
#00FF00 if transparency isn't possible), nothing touching the picture's edges unless the line says otherwise.

## 1. A second palm tree: `palm-2.png` (112 x 176)
> Draw a second jungle palm tree for a 16-bit Sonic style game, different from the first one: a trunk that leans to one
> side (or two trunks from one base), a full crown of fronds and a couple of coconuts. 112 x 176 pixels, transparent
> background. The trunk's base runs all the way to the bottom edge (it sinks into sloped ground), the crown has 8 px of
> space above it. Keep the trunk's foot centred at x 48.

The game mixes it in with the current palm (which it also mirrors and resizes now, so they don't repeat).

## 2. The near jungle strip: `jungle-near.png` (1024 x 320)
> The near layer of a 16-bit Sonic style jungle backdrop: a row of big jungle tree trunks with vines, ferns at the
> bottom, leafy canopies at the top. 1024 x 320 pixels, it repeats sideways (the left and right edges must join). The
> treetops must be WHOLE: leave 24 px of empty, see-through sky above the highest leaf. The area between the trunks is
> dark, solid foliage (no see-through gaps below the canopy).

The current strip has its canopies cut flat at the top edge, so for now the game shows the far treetops twice instead.

## 3. Tails tired frames (the `tired` row of the fox sheet, 2 frames)
> Redraw only the two "tired" frames of the three-tailed fox: hanging in the air after flying, tails drooping and
> slowly turning, arms down. TWO arms only (the current frames show three). Same size, colours and face circle as the
> fox's flying frames on the same sheet.

Until these come in, the game shows the flying frames with the tails turning slowly.

## 4. (Optional) the spin dash rows of both ball sheets
The dust in those frames runs into the cell edge, so it got cut off in straight lines. The game now draws its own dust
and uses the roll frames, so this one isn't needed unless you want sprite dust back.

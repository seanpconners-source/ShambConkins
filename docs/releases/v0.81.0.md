# ShambConkins v0.81.0: Sundown Showdown, friends online, items you can't miss, shared high scores

- **New game: Sundown Showdown**, a western run-and-gun for two at once on one screen. Each player drags in their half to aim and fire (8 ways), drags further to run while shooting, and taps to jump. Shoot bandits on Main Street, in the windows, on canyon ledges and around the mission at night; jump the knife men and the lit dynamite; grab Two Guns, Rapid Fire and money bags. At the end a WANTED boss wears a character you haven't unlocked: his bounty is split by the damage you each did, and beating him recruits him. One hit costs a life (3 each). With your Sunset Riders songs and voices. In 1P it's stage after stage, each one harder.
- **Items you can't miss**: using an item now fills the screen: the item zooms in over rays in its colour (red attacks, blue defence, green movement, gold points), its name slams in, then the result lands on each player in big numbers (points count up or down, the victim's face shakes, BLOCKED! when a shield or ward stops it, stamps like NEXT ROLL +3) with one plain sentence underneath. Tap to skip.
- **New board items**: roadblocks (drop one on your opponent's road: they stop dead on it), toll booths (they pay you every time they come through), coin drops (stacks on the road ahead, first to get there grabs 3 points) and the magnet (jump right to your opponent when they're ahead). 17 items that only gave a few points or a shield do these now: Cone of Dunshire, Banana Stand Cash, Cheesy Poofs, Iron Man Boots, Bungee Gum and more. Marks show on the board, and walking into one pops a big ROADBLOCK! / TOLL / COINS! stamp.
- **Friends online**: each phone has a friend code (shown in Play online). Anyone you play with in an online room becomes a friend by itself, or type a friend's code to add them (they get you back automatically). The friends list shows who has the game open: Free, Playing Pong, On the board. Tap Invite: if they're free it pops up on their phone at once; if they're in a game or mid-turn it waits until they're done. Join puts both phones in the same online room. The title screen's Online tile says how many friends are on.
- **Every menu box in the new console look**: Settings (rounded rows, real On/Off switches), the online room, confirm boxes, the pause card and the start and result cards now match the Arcade: glass cards, comic titles, a white pill for the main choice. Start and result cards show the game's own logo when it has one. Board dialogs keep each level's colours.
- **Pokémon moves that fit the character**: about 300 new moves (Stone Cold's STUNNER and BEER BONG, Ripley's POWER LOADER, Michael Myers' KITCHEN KNIFE, Hank's PROPANE, Ron Burgundy's JAZZ FLUTE, Mr. Burns' RELEASE DOGS, Cthulhu's R'LYEH RISING…). Every show now has its own moves, and almost every character carries two themed moves instead of one, plus one of its type and a status move. The dogs keep dog moves.
- **Shared high scores with names**: every 1-player run (and your side of an online duel) goes on the game's scoreboard under your Choose Characters name. A game's page shows the record and who holds it (🏆 chip); tap it for the top 10 from every phone, with faces. Offline, scores wait and go up the next time the game starts, and the board shows what this phone last saw. A new personal top-10 place pops up after a run.
- **39 more characters say their own famous line** (Johnny Cage, Ike, Uncle Jimbo, Majin Buu, Cell, Arthur Morgan, Mick Foley, Kenny Powers, Gordon Ramsay, Cthulhu and more), on their character page, unlock card and Pokémon intro.
- **Swipe through the Arcade**: on a game's page, swipe left for the next game and right for the previous one, anywhere on the picture, logo, text or buttons. The picture and text follow your finger and snap to the next game. Taps, the tile row and the category pills work as before.

Not tried on real phones yet: Sundown Showdown's feel and balance, friends online between two phones, the item cut-in. Shared high scores and friends online start working once this release's site update is live.

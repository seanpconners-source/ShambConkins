# v0.62.0 — Real Pinball: Bucketheadland, Black Sabbath, King Gizzard
## 🎸 Bucketheadland Pinball, rebuilt
A real pinball machine now: 3 balls each, players alternate ball by ball, no clock. 1P plays a whole game to beat the replay score.
- Flippers you aim by timing, pops, slings, drop targets, spinner, ramps and wireforms, kickback, plunger skill shot, ball save, tilt (flick up to nudge).
- The **Giant Robot** fights back in ROBOT BATTLE, the Coop kicks you to an upper flipper, the **Golden Egg** captive ball builds the jackpot, three locks start **JORDAN MULTIBALL**.
- Five timed attractions at the Haunted Ride lead to the **WELCOME TO BUCKETHEADLAND** wizard mode.
- Dot-matrix display with your face in dots, light shows, flashers, an original soundtrack and a high-score table.
- Buckethead is painted on the table and his strumming hand **is the left flipper**, under a flickering BUCKETHEADLAND sign.

## 🦇 Black Sabbath Pinball (new)
I-R-O-N lanes, WAR PIGS pops, an IRON MAN toy that walks toward you, the INTO THE VOID spinning disc, crossing PARANOID and ELECTRIC FUNERAL ramps, Graveyard locks for WAR PIGS MULTIBALL, the HAND OF DOOM post between the flippers, six album modes and the HEAVEN AND HELL wizard. Wins can recruit Ozzy, Iommi, Geezer or Bill Ward.

## 🦎 King Gizzard Pinball (new)
A mini playfield up top, **THE RATS' NEST**, with its own two flippers. The RATTLESNAKE swallows the ball and carries it up into the Nest, nine loops light **NONAGON INFINITY**, G-I-L-A drops light RATTLESNAKE MULTIBALL, the FLIGHT b741 ramp lights six album modes and the OMNIUM GATHERUM wizard. Band members join the roster.

## ⚙️ Table settings
Painted playfield, backglass and toy art on every table. A ⚙ button sets zoom (whole table ↔ full width), ball speed and auto zoom; a visible ⤢ button goes full screen. Tables run 15% slower, and a ball plunged too softly rolls back so you can pull again.

# v0.71.1 — Tony Hawk's Pro Mancala, and the app updates like the website
## 🛹 Tony Hawk's Pro Mancala
- Mancala is now a Tony Hawk game. Same rules, CPU and online play; everything you see and hear is new.
- Title sequence every time: Tony... Hawk's... Pro... Mancala... **EXTREME EDITION!!** (tap to skip).
- The board is a real skateboard deck with glass gems. It fills the screen, standing upright on tall phones.
- THPS2 screen: score and special meter, trick strings with multipliers, **COMBO!!!** when your last gem lands in your store, bails when you get robbed or set the other player up, special tricks.
- 90s fisheye camcorder cutaways to your skater: halfpipe, handrail, stair set, curb or kicker ramp. Grinds, airwalks, Christ airs, slams with a freeze-frame, hats, and a pose for the camera.
- Guest skaters from your roster go by all the time; crowd reactions and lots of rude popups (they fade back over the board while you're choosing a bowl).
- THPS soundtrack: one song from the title on, the next when it ends. Real Tony Hawk sound effects.
- End-of-run tally keeps your best trick score and best combo.
- The game file is about 200 MB now (the songs): update on Wi-Fi.
## 📲 Updates
- Opening the installed app now asks the site for a newer version and, if there is one, loads it right then (like the website does), saving it for offline play as it comes in. Unchanged: the saved copy opens at once. No signal: the saved copy after a few seconds.
- No more background download that started over whenever the app was closed part-way (with the ~200 MB game it could seem stuck).
- Coming back to an app that stayed open shows a **vX is out — Update** button.
- **Settings → Check for updates** never says "up to date" when it couldn't reach the site.
- Phones on an older version switch over by themselves: open the app, tap **Restart** when offered, and the new version loads.
## Also
- Sharp faces on the Pro Mancala player cards, skaters and pose shots.
- The Now Playing card no longer covers the camera labels during cutaways.

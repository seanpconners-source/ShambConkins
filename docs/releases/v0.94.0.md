# ShambConkins v0.94.0: Seanic playtest fixes, smoother loops, the old Sonic games retired

- **Seanic ground shows from the start:** the start of Act 2 (and anywhere the first view was mostly sky) could show no ground until you ran away and back. Fixed.
- **Loops feel like the real game:** running into a loop at a good speed always gets you round, and a fast roll does too; only a crawl falls back.
- **Faster acceleration:** you reach top speed in about 1.2 s instead of 2, and the spinning-legs dash shows as soon as you're really moving.
- **Warp tubes:** the camera glides along with you instead of jerking. The camera is also zoomed out a bit more.
- **Mini-boss:** its flames and smoke are drawn properly instead of being cut off in a square.
- **Monkey robots** climb a real palm tree, and the crumbling bridge in Act 1 drops its own planks.
- **Corkscrews:** the runner barrel-rolls round the track instead of spinning like a wheel.
- **Sizes:** the fox's ball and the hedgehog's quills are a little smaller.
- **Act 2:** the monitor buried in the hill at the start (and two rings in the tube hill) are back above ground.
- **Title screen:** on tall phones the two palms are whole instead of cut off at the top.
- **The old Sonic games are retired** (Green Hill Boss Act, Goal Dash and the engine Test Zone): Seanic replaces them. The Green Hill warp zone now deals Seanic Acts 1 and 2. Old saves still load.
- **Symphony of the Night:** the sword swipes are flatter (they tilted too far down).

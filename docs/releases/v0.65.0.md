# v0.65.0 — Board Game on Two Phones
## 🎲 Play the board game together, each on your own phone
- Title screen → **🌐 PLAY ONLINE** (or Arcade → **🌐 Online**) → both phones type the **same room name**.
- Tap **🎲 Board game together**. The other phone gets an invite; tap **Play** and both phones open New Game.
- Each phone picks its own character (you see the other pick live). The phone that invited picks the board and length and taps **Start on both phones**.

## How it plays
- The inviting phone runs the game and keeps the save. The other phone shows the same board, live.
- On your turn your phone rolls, picks roads, uses items, answers your cards and plays your Pokémon battles. The other phone watches it happen (dice, cards, banners, warps).
- Minigames: both phones play at the same time from the same start (the 1-player version), and the higher score wins on the board. For now only games that work online are dealt (trivia, casino and CPU games come next).
- New characters you win go to your own phone's roster.
- If a phone loses signal the game waits for it, or the other phone can play that turn. **Home** on the second phone leaves the game (its turns are then played on the host phone); **Rejoin** in the room brings it back.
- Both phones need this same version: close and reopen the app on each.

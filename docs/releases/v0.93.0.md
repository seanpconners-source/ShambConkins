# ShambConkins v0.93.0: Seanic Act 2, the Act 1 mini-boss, and a pile of fixes

- **Seanic the Hedgehog · Shamb Island Zone Act 2** (new in the Arcade), to the Emerald Hill Zone song. Two corkscrews (keep your speed up or you drop to the road below), waterfall cliffs with red springs, a warp tube through a hill, a lagoon bridge with leaping robot fish, seesaws that fire you up (land on the high end), diagonal springs up to a treetop high route with a zip line down, swinging platforms over a pit, a double loop, a spike log on a bridge and a fourth loop before the sign.
- **A mini-boss at the end of Act 1**, to the Sonic 3 Miniboss song: a flame pod in a one-screen arena. It hovers, sets down and sweeps flames along the floor both ways, then sits smoking for a moment: that's when to jump on it. Six hits wrecks it and frees its pilot, a character you haven't unlocked yet, into your roster. That pilot is now the unlock in Seanic games (no random unlocks there any more).
- **Seanic looks:** the hedgehog is a little smaller and the fox matched to him; both balls the same size, with your face as big as when standing. Walking uses real strides (proper walk frames are on the art to-do list). Grass now runs in front of each loop's entrance. The title screen gets a sky with drifting clouds on tall phones instead of a flat blue block; the character select background fills the screen.
- **Uno:** no more being dealt the same hand every game (every game in the Arcade now deals fresh), and games no longer stop suddenly at 80 moves with "lower card value wins" (the limit is 300, with a countdown near the end).
- **Drag race:** a red light no longer counts twice, so whoever crosses the line first wins.
- **Baseball:** a ball caught before it bounces is always an out; a home run is called the moment it clears the fence, the fielders stop chasing it and the runners trot round quickly.
- **Chutes and Ladders · Spinner Showdown:** the CPU takes its turns.
- **Symphony of the Night:** the fence in the opening no longer disappears.
- **Online** stays on the home screen: New Game in a room with a friend starts the board game together, the Arcade has the challenges.
- **NEW HIGH SCORE!** badge on the result card when you beat your best.
- **Arcade:** 🎲 picks from every game whatever shelf you're on; Strategema and Four Lights have their own tiles; Pong is pulled out a bit.

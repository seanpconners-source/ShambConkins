# ShambConkins v0.85.0: casino tables that explain themselves, real roulette, baseball you can play, no Arcade flicker

- **The casino sounds like a casino app now.** The constant static under every table (a looped noise "crowd") is gone, and so is the hissy drum loop. Instead there's quiet lounge music (a warm electric piano over a walking upright bass, jazz chords, a soft room) well under clean, short effects: clay chips clacking, cards swishing and landing, a crisp flip, the roulette ball rolling, slowing, bouncing over the frets and dropping, a bell chime for a win and a coin shower for a big one, a soft low note for a loss. MUSIC and SOUNDS switches on every table's ? sheet.
- **Every casino table now explains itself.** Blackjack, baccarat and five-card draw open on the same welcome card as roulette (the dealer and regulars by name, the goal, how to play in three steps, RULES & PAYOUTS). A first-time tour points at what to tap. A banner at the top of the controls always says whose turn it is and what to do ("AMBER — $20 TO CALL · CALL to stay in, RAISE to push, FOLD to give up", "MONEYPENNY TURNS THE PLAYER CARDS", "SEAN WINS $50 · AMBER LOSES $25"). There's a ? button for the full rules, and a summary card at the end of the session.
- **Roulette, rebuilt as a real game.** A welcome card first: the table, the croupier and tonight's regulars by name, the goal, how to bet in three steps. A guided tour the first time (pick a chip, tap the board, spin). The full European board with every real bet: straights, splits, streets, corners, six lines, the zero trios and first four, dozens, columns, red/black, odd/even, 1-18/19-36. Hold a finger to see what a spot covers and what it pays. UNDO, CLEAR, REBET, ×2 and a big SPIN. The wheel comes up big for the spin, the croupier calls the number, winners are paid on the spot with their odds shown, losers are swept. Table history with hot numbers, everyone's chips on show, a ? sheet with every payout, and a summary at the end. Two on one phone bet in turn; online you both bet at once.
- **The Arcade no longer flickers when you flick between games.** Your screen recording showed the phone running out of graphics memory: every tap added another full-screen background with its own blurred copy and an endless slow zoom, and tapping fast stacked them until Chrome blanked the screen. Now there are exactly two background slots, reused, with a short fade; no live blur, no endless zoom, no animated colour wash; the text swaps in one go once its logo is ready. Measured on a slowed phone-size browser: page work per move 350 ms → 54 ms, and the stacked layers stay flat (13 screens' worth instead of growing to 24 when tapping fast).
- **Baseball plays like the real game now.** Batters move around the batter's box: closer to the plate reaches the outside pitch, and up in the box hits a curve before it breaks. Tap SWING as the ball arrives and flick up to lift it or down to hit it on the ground, or square around to BUNT. Pitchers move along the rubber, pick a pitch and steer it while it flies: curves bend most up and down, sliders sideways. Two players on one phone split the screen: the pitcher has the top half, the batter the bottom.
- **A proper bat**: tapered with a knob, cocked behind the helmet, and swinging through the zone and over the shoulder.
- **More real at-bats**: the CPU pitches more off the plate and takes more pitches, and more swings go foul, so counts build and walks happen.

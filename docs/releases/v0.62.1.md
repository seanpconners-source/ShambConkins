# v0.62.1 — Pinball on iPad
## 🍎 Pinball now launches on iPad
The three pinball tables (Bucketheadland, Black Sabbath, King Gizzard) wouldn't start on an iPad: they pre-drew the table at a size that ran past iPad Safari's limit on picture memory. They now use a little less memory, free the old picture before drawing a new one (on rotation or when the art loads), and fall back to a lighter picture if Safari still says no. Phones look and play the same.

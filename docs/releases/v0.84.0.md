# ShambConkins v0.84.0: real baseball, the Vegas casino, 79 item pictures

- **79 more items have their own picture** (from the batch 04 and 05 uploads): the Phoenix Down, the materia orb, the White Russian, the Dude's bathrobe, the Mrs. Doubtfire oven gloves, the Warriors bottle, the subway token, the Klobb, the proximity mine, Toasty!, the Fatality scroll, the Timecop badge, Freddy's sweater, the Soup Nazi's ladle, the Blue Steel dryer, City Wok, Tweek's coffee, Salty Balls and more. 303 items now have one.
- **Sundown Showdown's bosses wear your sprite sheet**: the boss sheet you made (long black coat, red lining) replaces the drawn boss.
- **Baseball is ready for your own sprites**: batters, pitchers, fielders, the catcher, the umpire, helmets and caps, the ball, the stadium, the outfield wall and both logos each take a picture. Anything not made yet is still drawn by the game. The brief for ChatGPT is `docs/baseball-sprites/GPT-SPRITE-BRIEF.md`.
- **Smaller installed-app page**: item pictures are now separate app files instead of being packed inside the page, which had grown too big for the server (9 MB now, was 27 MB).

- **Major League Baseball is a real ballgame now** (was Bat & Field), in the style of Ken Griffey Jr. baseball on the SNES. Pick a fastball, curve, slider or change-up and tap where to throw it; batting, hold a finger to aim the bat and let go to swing. Balls in play cut to the field: the nearest fielder runs to the ball (drag to steer him), catches flies, and you tap a base to throw; GO and BACK send or hold your runners. Balls, strikes, walks, force and tag outs, extra innings and walk-offs. 1, 3 or 9 innings in the Arcade, one inning on the board. Your roster bats, filled out by the Major League cast, and Harry Doyle calls the game.
- **The Batting Cage is a Home Run Derby** in the same look: ten outs each, most home runs wins.
- **Nine new characters from Major League:** Rick “Wild Thing” Vaughn, Jake Taylor, Willie Mays Hayes, Pedro Cerrano, Roger Dorn, Eddie Harris, Lou Brown, Harry Doyle and Rachel Phelps. Win the baseball games to recruit them.

- **No more pinch-zoom on menus and in games.** Two fingers no longer zoom the page anywhere (menus, the Arcade, every game). The board map still zooms with a pinch, as before.
- **Swiping between games in the Arcade no longer flashes.** The old game slides away where your finger let go and the new one slides in, instead of everything jumping back and blinking.

- **Blackjack is a real Vegas table now.** A drawn casino: felt with the printed rules, a padded rail, the chip rack and the card shoe, a dealer from the table's movie or show (Vesper at Casino Royale, Elvis at Viva Las Vegas, Mr. Chow at the Wolfpack, Quark at Quark's) and regulars in the other seats who play their own hands. Real rules: $1,000 each, $25 to $500 a hand, a 3-deck shoe with a cut card (that hand is the last), insurance, doubling, splits, dealer stands on all 17s. Tap chips to bet, tap the felt to hit, swipe sideways to stand. Crowd noise and an original lounge-jazz loop.
- **Baccarat, five-card draw and roulette moved to the Vegas tables too.** Baccarat at the Casino Royale table: bet Player, Banker or Tie, and the biggest bettor squeezes the cards (drag them up to peel), with the real third-card rules and a scoreboard of the coups. Five-card draw: ante, bet, draw up to three, bet again, showdown, with regulars playing their own hands; on one phone your cards stay face down until you tap SHOW. Roulette: a spinning wheel and ball, the real betting layout, your own chip colour. All play vs the CPU and online.
- **Break the bank and the dealer joins you.** Finish any casino game with $1,500 or more and the dealer is impressed. Elvis Presley and Mr. Chow can only be recruited this way; Alan Garner is a new character too.

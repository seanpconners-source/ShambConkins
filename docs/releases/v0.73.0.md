# v0.73.0 — Bucketheadland plays Buckethead
## 🎸 Bucketheadland pinball
- **Real Buckethead songs.** Seven of them: the shred plays from the title on (Welcome to Bucketheadland, Nottingham Lace, Jordan, Soothsayer), modes and multiball jump to the craziest parts, the mellow ones only play at game over.
- **Music that follows the game.** A song keeps playing so you get into the groove and only changes when the game does. JORDAN MULTIBALL plays Jordan, Soothsayer plays its closing solo, Chicken Coop Panic plays Nottingham Lace, the wizard mode plays Welcome to Bucketheadland. Songs crossfade and start right away; a song you left comes back where it was.
- **Guitar sounds on the whole table.** Amp switching on, rev-ups, pick scrapes, metal sparks, string scratches, wails, an alarm for DANGER and the amp unplugged on a TILT. Solos for a lost ball, super jackpots and game over. Every sound sits under the music with a short reverb tail.

## 🌐 Online pinball
- **Takes turns like a real machine** on all three tables: player 1's ball on their phone while the other phone watches it live (same table, lights, display, sounds and song), then player 2's ball, 3 balls each.
- **Both scores on screen** (who's up, ball numbers, who leads and by how much), also in local 2-player games.

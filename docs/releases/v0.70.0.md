# v0.70.0 — 327 more faces, no more doubles, 19 new characters
## 🎭 327 more characters have their own pictures
Most come with several faces (win, sad, angry, think, silly, walk): the Kill Bill, Pulp Fiction, Reservoir Dogs, Jackie Brown, Inglourious Basterds and Django casts, Red Dead Redemption, the Power Rangers, the Ninja Turtles, Mortal Kombat, the Goonies, the Breakfast Club, Seinfeld's regulars, South Park, Star Trek, Star Wars, the Godfather family, Bond villains, horror icons and many more. Dr. No, Jackie Moon, Ash Ketchum and Dr. Robotnik now have a main picture, so their other faces show too. Cedric and Omar's mixed-up faces are fixed.

## 👯 No more doubles
45 characters were in the game twice or three times under different names (the Terminator three times, Doc Brown, Marty, Ace Ventura, Michael Scott, Dwight, the Bond and Mortal Kombat pairs and more). Each is now one character. Anyone you'd unlocked under the other name is still in your roster, now as the kept one.

## 🆕 19 new characters
- **Little Shop of Horrors:** Audrey, Mr. Mushnik, Orin Scrivello DDS and the street-corner girls Crystal, Ronnette and Chiffon.
- **The Naked Gun:** Leslie Nielsen, Frank Drebin, Nordberg and O.J. Simpson, at a new landmark, Police Squad! Headquarters.
- **Married... with Children:** Al, Peggy, Kelly, Bud and Buck the dog, at a new landmark, The Bundy House.
- Charles Manson (Once Upon a Time in Hollywood), Adolf Hitler (Inglourious Basterds), Barack Obama (Key & Peele) and Bill Clinton (Saturday Night Live).

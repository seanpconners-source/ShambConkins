# ShambConkins v0.87.0: chat, your Castlevania monsters, Griffey-style batting

- **Chat with the other phone.** When you're online together, a 💬 button opens quick chat: a few lines for the moment, three nice ones, four nasty ones (two properly filthy), emotes, a line from your character, or type your own. You get a fresh hand every time. Messages pop up with the sender's face. It works in rooms, on the board game, during online minigames, and with any online friend. Settings → Clean chat drops the swearing.
- **Online turn games feel more immediate.** The other player's move shows up right away. Pro Mancala cuts its cutaway short when their move is waiting, and every online game confirms and resends moves faster.
- **Symphony of the Night uses your sprites** for Death, Dracula and his demon form, the armor knight, mermen, wargs, the Bloody Zombie, skeletons, zombies, bats and candelabras, plus the six floors, moon, bridge, balconies, bars, cracked walls, windows, pillars, arches, statue and throne. The title screen uses your logo with a new lightning intro.
- **Batting looks like Ken Griffey Jr. baseball.** It has the SNES camera behind the plate, the clay and both batter's boxes, and a HUD like the game's. The pitch comes all the way into the catcher's mitt. Your new batter sheets are in, with a full-length bat and the face on the shoulders.

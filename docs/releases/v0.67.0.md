# v0.67.0 — Every head-to-head game plays for real on two phones
## 🌐 Online duels and the online board game: real head-to-head play
Until now, a game with a CPU opponent couldn't be played online: each phone just played its own solo version and compared scores. Now all 95 of them are played for real between the two phones, in an Arcade 🌐 Online duel and in the online board game.
- **Turn-based games** (chess, checkers, Tri-D chess, Connect 4, Reversi, the tic-tac-toes, Mancala, Dots & Boxes, Uno, Yahtzee, the casino tables, Guess Who, Battleship, Liar's Dice, Clue, Monopoly, trivia, Hangman, N-Spade...): each phone plays its own turns and watches the other player's moves as they happen. Your own hand, secret character or fleet never shows on the other phone.
- **Race and reaction games** (Clue Lineup, Hangman Quick Draw, Mexican Standoff, Skynet...): each phone times your own taps by its own clock, so a slow connection never costs you.
- **Shot games** (Beer Pong, curling, artillery, putt-putt, nine-ball, BASEketball): the shooter's phone plays the shot and the other phone watches it land.
- **Side-by-side games** (Tetris, Shred Hero, Stacker, drag race, Twist Contest, Bounty Hunter, Cheesy Poofs, the Bond games...): your side fills most of the screen and the other player's runs beside it.
- **Arena games** (Pong, TRON, slither, tanks, TowerFall, Battle Royale, Bomber, Pac-Man, sword duels, snowball fight, dodgeball, air hockey, sumo, Crazy 88, Stunt Chase, Space Invaders, Hyrule, Fight Night, Sewer Clash, penalty kicks...): your player moves the instant you touch, and your phone decides what hits you from what you see on your screen. Each phone turns the arena so you start at the bottom.
- **Mario Kart Grand Prix VS and Balloon Battle:** each phone races its own kart full screen and sees the other kart live; Player 1's phone runs the CPU racers.
- **Space Invaders Co-op:** two humans on one team, winning or losing together.
## 🧳 Meet a visitor works at every landmark
Landmarks from a film or show with no characters of its own now send a visitor from the same kind of property (or anyone you're still missing) instead of only "Catch up, +3 points".
## Notes
Everything was tested with two simulated phones, with and without extra network delay. It has not been tried on two real phones yet.

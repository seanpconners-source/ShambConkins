# ShambConkins v0.83.0: Sundown Showdown rebuilt

- **Sundown Showdown, rebuilt from the ground up**: four full stages played in order: MAIN STREET, RUNAWAY TRAIN (fight along the roofs of a moving train, jump the gaps between cars, duck under low bridges, riders gallop alongside), CANYON PASS (climb rock shelves and a rope bridge, rockslides of boulders you jump or shoot) and MISSION AT NIGHT (torch-lit adobe streets up to a bell tower). Every stage is layered: balconies and rooftops you jump up onto, wagons, barrels and crates to stand on and hide behind.
- **Things happen all the time**: gunmen in windows, on balconies and roofs, bursting out of doors, dropping off roofs into the street, hiding behind crates, knife men, dynamite throwers, snipers with a red aiming line, horseback riders, and set pieces: a STAMPEDE of runaway horses (get up high), rolling powder kegs (shoot them and everything around goes up), a GATLING WAGON mini-boss, SHOWDOWNS that hold the screen until the gang is down. When the street goes quiet, more outlaws turn up.
- **Four different bosses**, each wearing a WANTED character you haven't unlocked: a gunslinger on Main Street, the engineer on the locomotive (dynamite bundles, coal raining down, then he jumps onto the train), a rifleman leaping between high rock shelves, and the general fighting from the bell tower.
- **Arcade controls**: a floating stick and FIRE and JUMP buttons for each player, drawn on the screen. Hold FIRE to keep shooting, hold JUMP to jump higher, down + JUMP to slide under bullets or drop off a balcony. Aim 8 ways. Quick, snappy running and jumping. Keyboards and gamepads work too.
- **Shoot everything**: windows shatter, crates and barrels splinter (some hide money bags or guns), red powder kegs explode. Fast kills chain a combo multiplier (×2 up to ×8).
- **Your sprites are in**: both cowboys, the bandits, knife men, dynamite men, window gunmen, riders, horses, wagon, hats, items, the WANTED poster and the title logo. The whole world is drawn as pixel art at the same size as your sprites, so it all looks like one 16-bit game; faces stay sharp.
- **The 2P Arcade plays the whole campaign** (stage after stage until you're both out); a board-game round is one stage.

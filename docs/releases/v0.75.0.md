# v0.75.0 — Boss fights get serious
## 💥 Boss fights, rebuilt
- **Parry and revive work**: they react the moment you touch the screen (they used to wait for your finger to lift). A slightly early tap still parries; revive by tapping near your partner's bubble or just staying on it.
- **Four tiers: Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme**. Level I bosses are Easy, Level II Medium, Level III Hard, Level IV Extreme; warp zone bosses match the level you warped in from. Hard and Extreme add a FINAL PHASE.
- **Boss Select ladder**: pick a tier when you replay a boss, beat it to open the next. Medals on every boss card, a gold frame for beating Extreme.
- **New attacks**: bosses ambush from the bottom or the sides, lob bombs at marked spots and carpet-bomb the screen. Their bullets are things from their own world (Biff throws manure, Dracula bats, King Kandy candy).
- **Faces with expressions**: cut-out heads that gloat, laugh, get hurt and sulk, instead of a photo in a circle.

## 🔥 Explosions and chatter
- **Knockouts scale with the tier**: an Extreme knockout fills the whole screen (ANNIHILATED!!), and the boss's head goes flying.
- **Phases look different**: armour blows off, the boss smokes, burns and glows, the screen turns red.
- **Star Fox-style radio**: a comm box with the speaker's face. Boss and players trade lines between phases, shout when someone is hit or goes down, and get special lines when your character comes from the boss's own movie or show.

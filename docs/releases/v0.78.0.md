# v0.78.0 — Every menu gets the console look
## 🕹️ Arcade
- **Categories that make sense**: Action, Retro Arcade, Head to Head, Racing & Sports, Board & Cards, Puzzle, Party, Trivia & Game Shows, Casino & Dice, Pinball, Co-op, Bosses, Pokémon.
- **View all**: every game as big blocks you scroll through; tap one to jump to it.
- Fits small phones better, and **Play starts the game straight away** (no more Start / Back to arcade card).

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Characters & New Game
- **Characters** has its own screen: big blocks, search, each player's slot shows their character cycling through its moods.
- **New Game** is clear now: your two players (tap *Change characters*), big mode cards that explain themselves, START AT level cards, board and seed. No character picking there any more.

## 🎭 Collection
- Big blocks for every character. Tap one for its page: all its moods, other versions, boss medals and tier looks, catchphrase, perk, and **Play as Sean / Amber**.

## ✨ Title screen
- The tiles come alive: your characters cycle their faces, the Collection and Arcade tiles slide through characters and games, the Online tile shows two phones talking.

## 🎨 Art Kit
- Every game has two picture slots (big picture + icon). The Art Kit has a ready-made prompt for each, and a tool that files the pictures for you.

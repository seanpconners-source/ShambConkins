# v0.66.0 — Play Alone vs a CPU
## 🤖 1 player board game
- New Game → **1 Player vs CPU**, pick who you are (Sean or Amber) and the CPU level (Easy / Normal / Hard). This is the same setting as the Minigame Vault's 1P switch.
- You go first. The CPU wears a head from the other roster, named like "Dennis (CPU)".
- On its turn the CPU rolls by itself, sometimes uses an item, picks its roads and its own choices. You see its card greyed out with "… is thinking", then its pick lights up. Its Pokémon battles play themselves.
- You still tap Continue on its cards so you can see what happened, and you decide what you both do together: starting a minigame, fighting a boss, fighting again.
- Minigames are the ones with a real CPU opponent (about 110: trivia, cards, chess, Pong, duels…), plus boss fights and the zombie horde, which you play alone. Other games are swapped out.
- The CPU's wins don't add characters to anyone's roster. Versus and Horror modes.

## 🎭 Choose Characters on the title screen
- New **CHOOSE CHARACTERS** button: pick names and heads once. They are saved and used by every New Game and every Minigame Vault game.
- The title screen shows your picks; New Game still lets you change them.

# v0.68.1 — DOOM: bigger faces, Android-style buttons
## 👹 DOOM and DOOM II
- **Bigger faces on the monsters:** the roster faces the monsters wear are now twice as big.
- **Touch buttons like the official DOOM Android app:** a crosshair FIRE button on the right and a second one above the move stick, an open-hand USE button next to it, a pistol button to switch to the next weapon, a map pin for the automap, and the move stick resting bottom-left (it still starts wherever your left thumb lands).
- **Your face in the status bar** is a little bigger, so it fills its box.
## Notes
Nothing else changed. Tested in a headless browser at phone and desktop sizes; not yet tried on a real phone.

# v0.77.0 — The new Arcade: a console-style home for every game
## 🕹️ The Arcade
- **One big game at a time**: the selected game fills the screen with its picture (glowing outline), its own colour and its description; scroll the row of tiles along the bottom to pick another.
- **Versions live together**: Broforce's four missions, the three pinball tables, Mario, Mario Kart, Castlevania, Trivia Night, Casino Night, Boss Select (every boss, the ones you haven't beaten in shadow) and more each have ONE tile that opens a page of version tiles.
- Category pills on top, search, 🎲 random game, 1 Player / 2 Players / Online all still there.

## ✨ New look
- Navy, white and electric blue on the title screen and menus, with comic-book titles.
- Everything moves: screens slide in, the colour blends from game to game, pictures glide in the direction you scroll, version tiles cascade.
- The character pictures are placeholders: every game has two picture slots (a big picture and a tile icon) waiting for real art. The pinball tables already use their backglass art.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 New characters
- 43 new characters: wrestlers (Stone Cold, the Undertaker, Mick Foley and his personas), the Idiocracy cast, Reno 911!, the Comedy Bang Bang regulars, The Birthday Boys and more.

## 🧪 Behind the scenes
- **SOAK-TEST.cmd** for the laptop: plays the long tests over and over until the window is closed and keeps a report in soak-reports/.

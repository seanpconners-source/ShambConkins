# v0.68.0 — Play together online: one shared world on two phones
## 🌐 Online, the take-turns games become one game played at the same time
Online, these games used to be two separate solo runs with the scores compared at the end. Now both of you play in one shared world, each on your own phone with your own camera. When your partner is off screen, an arrow on the edge of the screen points to them. Played on one phone, these games are exactly as before.
- **Super Mario Bros. World 1 (co-op):** Mario and Luigi play the stages together. Blocks, coins, power-ups and enemies are shared. Lose a life and you float back next to your partner in a bubble. Out of lives, you watch until the next stage and come back with one life. The first one to the flagpole gives the other 10 seconds, then pulls them along, and you start each stage together. **Coin Rush** and **Castle Dash** are live races in the same world.
- **Sonic (co-op):** Sonic and Tails run Green Hill Act 3 together and beat the boss as a team; its pilot joins both rosters. **Goal Dash** is a live race to the signpost.
- **Broforce (co-op, all four missions):** both bros fight through the mission and take on the Devil together. Blown-up terrain, cages and checkpoints are shared, and a GOLD cage frees its character for both of you. Free a prisoner while your partner is out of bros and they come back. The chopper waits 10 seconds for the second bro.
- **Castlevania (co-op):** in Vampire Killer you go through the castle together and fight the Phantom Bat; Night of the Candles is a live hunt for hearts in the same castle. **Symphony of the Night:** both heroes storm the castle and face Dracula together.
- **Every boss fight (co-op):** both planes fight the boss at once. In the online board game this includes the bosses at every level gate.
- **Return of the Living Dead / Evil Dead horde (co-op):** survive the night together. Level-ups don't pause the night online: while you pick a card you stand still and nothing can hurt you.
- **The Warriors (co-op):** both Warriors walk the same street and fight the gangs together.
- **DOOM and DOOM II (co-op):** both marines in the same level, like the 1993 network game: keys are shared, you get 3 lives each, and you come back next to your partner. Both phones need the same WAD (or both use Freedoom).
- **Mario Kart:** online, **Time Trials** becomes a 1-on-1 race with just your two karts and items on, and **Grand Prix (pass & play)** becomes one race together with the CPU racers.
- **Photo Hunt:** both of you hunt the same photos at the same time. Whoever taps a difference first gets it.
## Notes
Scores and winners work as before: your own score, and the higher one wins. Everything was tested with two simulated phones. It has not been tried on two real phones yet.

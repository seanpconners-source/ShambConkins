# ShambConkins v0.80.8: backgrounds fit every phone, Restart button, Hangman music that follows the game, updates that come through

- **Arcade backgrounds on every phone**: on a screen that isn't one of the Background Picker's (most phones), the game takes the picker screen closest in shape and moves your setting over by the layout: Sean and Amber keep the same gap above the text and the same share of the width, and on a shorter screen the empty room above them is used up first before they get smaller. Before, the setting was only moved round the middle of the screen, so on shorter phones the faces slid under the text. Checked on 16 phone sizes (iPhone SE to Pro Max, Samsung, Pixel, Fold).
- **Amber's Samsung (360x780) and Sean's closed Fold (360x840) are in the Background Picker** (upright and sideways), ready to lock.
- **↻ Restart on the pause screen**: every game's pause card has Restart next to Exit. After a quick "Restart this game?" the same game starts again from the beginning with the same players and settings (no Start card). On the board the turn still waits for that game. Not in online games (both phones would have to agree).
- **The small grey genre line** (RUN AND GUN · ACTION) now sits under the game's logo, above the chips, on every screen, instead of across the faces.
- **Hangman Unchained music follows the game** (like Bucketheadland's): calm Sunset Riders songs while a bounty is being written, the stage themes while a phrase is being shot at, and a boss/showdown song as soon as someone is down to their last two bullets or Quick Draw's clock drops under 20 seconds. Songs crossfade and play out to the end; it only goes back to a calmer song after things have stayed calm for 8 seconds (and the song has had at least 15), and then picks up the song it left where it was.
- **All 20 Genesis songs were silent**: their volume in the level table had been rounded down to 0, so only SNES songs ever played. Levels re-measured; both versions now play at the same loudness.
- **The synth Yeehaw is gone**: the game opens and cheers with the real Sunset Riders voices.
- **Updates on slow networks**: Update now downloads the new version first, with a progress bar, and restarts only when it is in. Before, the restart waited only 2.5 seconds for the site and then opened the saved old copy, which is why it kept "not updating" on some Wi-Fi. When the app opens the old copy because the site was slow and the new one arrives a bit later, it now offers Restart. Settings → Check for updates says what went wrong and how long it took. The site also starts sending the page at once instead of after fetching it whole.

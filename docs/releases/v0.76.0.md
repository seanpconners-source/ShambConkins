# v0.76.0 — Frank's real game, two endings, and bosses with power-ups
## 🧪 Level IV and the endings
- **Frank's card plays it out**: Frank starts Shits and Ladders, you both take the "laxative", the board melts away… *I don't think that was a laxative.* Frank's face, then straight into **Level IV: Horror** from the middle of Level III.
- **Level IV looks the part**: rusty floor plates, blood dripping, chains and meat hooks, fire along the edges.
- **Two endings**: finish at the Level III boss and you get a suspiciously cheap one. Beat Level IV and you get the real one: dawn, your two characters, some very made-up lore about them, and credits from your run. Both can be skipped.
- **Checkpoints**: once you've reached a level, New Game lets you START AT it (later starts begin with an empty bag).

## 💥 Boss fights
- **Power-ups** drift down mid-fight: spread shot, turbo, homing missiles, shield, smart bomb, extra heart.
- **New ships**, and when your partner goes down: slow motion, a close-up of your crying face and a big **NOOOOOO!!!!**
- Drop-in slots for boss music (send songs!).

## 🎮 Games
- **Bomber duel**: joystick controls, smooth walking that slides round corners, bomb button, little Bomberman bodies.
- **Pac-Maze Duel**: real arcade ghost brains and speed, the classic maze look and sounds.
- **Fixes**: T-800 chase (and Breakout) in 1P no longer run absurdly fast; Color Card Dash stops saying "Too Early"; Hole.it watermelons drop when they fit and finished goals leave the screen; King Gizzard pinball balls no longer get stuck in the Rats' Nest; smoother character-select heads.
- **No more run length**: every run goes to the finish.

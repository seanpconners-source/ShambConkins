Backgrounds starring Sean & Amber, logos and tile icons for more than 30 games, and the big background now keeps you both in view on any screen.

- **Arcade art** for Major League, 007, Boss Select, Candy Land, Casino Night, Castlevania, Clue, Monopoly, Pac-Maze Duel, Paddy's Pub, Microgame Rush, Mario Kart, Chutes and Ladders, DOOM, DOOM II, Bucketheadland, Black Sabbath and King Gizzard pinball, Trivia Night, Mighty Ducks, The Matrix, Terminator, Zelda, Taskmaster, Tarantino Night, Star Trek, Space Invaders, Sonic, Super Mario Bros. and South Park, plus a new Sean of a Gun icon.
- **Backgrounds never cut you off**: the picture moves and zooms so you both stay in view on phones, tablets and desktops.
- **Title screen**: the Arcade tile cycles through recently played and featured games, in a new order every time; smoother tile pictures.
- **Faces show at once** on the first game's pass card.
- **Tony Hawk's Pro Mancala**: the EXTREME EDITION banner fits on phones; brighter title words.
